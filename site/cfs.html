FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still wondering about something? Text us and a coach will answer.

Never done CrossFit?

Book a free class. A coach meets you 10 minutes early, shows you around and scales every movement for you.

When is the gym open?

During coached classes and weekend open gym. See the schedule above for this week's times.

Who gets a free drop in?

Anyone who trains CrossFit somewhere else. Visiting family in Milford? Come work out with us on the house.

What should I bring?

Water, training shoes and clothes you can move in. We have everything else.
